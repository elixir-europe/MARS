Study Protocol Description			The overall process of collecting multi-omics samples is illustrated in Figure 1. Flash-frozen Daphnia samples from the exposure assays were processed using an automated workflow on a liquid-handling robot (i7, Beckman Coulter). For each sample, methanol/water (71.1:28.9 %v/v, 780 µL, Optima LC-MS grade methanol and water, Thermo Fisher Scientific) was added to CK14 Precellys homogenisation tubes (Stretton Scientific) prior to homogenisation with a bead-based homogeniser (Geno/Grinder 2020, Spex; 1750 rpm, 60 s). 144 μL of the resulting homogenate was flash frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at –80 °C for RNA extraction.The remaining homogenate was moved to a 96-well plate (1 mL 96-deep well plate, Eppendorf) and 85 µL chloroform (HPLC Plus chloroform, Sigma Aldrich) was added and then the sample was vortexed (1100 rpm, 90 seconds) before centrifugation (4000 x g, 4 °C, 10 minutes) to remove the protein precipitate and cell debris. 530 µL of the supernatant was removed and added to another 1 mL 96-well plate, containing 277 µL chloroform and 171 µL water. Samples were then vortex mixed (900 rpm, 30 seconds) and incubated at 4 °C for 5 minutes and then centrifuged (4000 x g, 4°C, 10 minutes). After centrifugation, samples were incubated at 4 °C for 10-minutes. 505 µL of the upper (polar) and 245 µL of the lower (non-polar) layers were removed, dried at 35 °C (polar dried in a SpeedVac concentrator, Savant SPD111V230, Thermo Fisher Scientific; non-polar dried under nitrogen gas at room temperature using a Techne FSC400D, Thermo Fisher Scientific) and stored at -80 °C until analysis. Process blank samples are created by using exactly the same procedure above, but with no Daphnia tissue included.		Total RNA extraction was conducted on the Biomek FxP workstation (Beckman Coulter, A31842) using the Agencourt RNAdvance Tissue Total RNA kit (Beckman Coulter), following the manufacturer’s guidelines. mRNA libraries were prepared from the extracted RNA using the NEBNext Ultra Directional RNA Library Prep Kit (New England Biolabs, E7420L) using the NEBNext Multiplex Oligos for Illumina Dual Index Primers (New England Biolabs, E7600S). Library preparation was completed on the Biomek FxP workstation (Beckman Coulter, A31842). The concentration, integrity, and purity of the extracted total RNA and mRNA libraries were assessed using the Nanodrop 8000 Spectrophotometer (Labtech Ltd., U.K.) and the TapeStation 2200 (Agilent Technologies), respectively. All 180 libraries were pooled to a final concentration of 14 nM and sequenced across five lanes, aiming for 5-7 million reads per sample. Sequencing was conducted using a 100-bp paired-end strategy on a DNBseq platform by EnviSion BioSequencing and BioComputing at the University of Birmingham.
